Powerful Features for Modern Blogging

Everything you need to manage your Jekyll blog from one beautiful interface

Visual Editor

Create and format content with an intuitive, distraction-free editor. Switch between visual, markdown, and split-preview modes.

GitHub Native

Seamlessly sync with your GitHub repository. Commit, push, and deploy with zero configuration. Your workflow, your way.

✨

AI Writing Assistant

Scheduled Publishing

Plan ahead and automate your publishing schedule. Set it and forget it—posts publish exactly when you want them.

Real-time Preview

Three-Mode Editor for Every Workflow

Whether you prefer visual editing, raw markdown, or split-view comparison, Jekyll Forge adapts to your style. Switch modes instantly without losing your work.

  • Visual Mode: Intuitive formatting toolbar, drag-and-drop media, WYSIWYG editing
  • Markdown Mode: Raw markdown editing with syntax highlighting and live validation
  • Split Preview: Edit on the left, preview on the right with real-time rendering

Get Started in Minutes

Three simple steps to start managing your Jekyll blog visually

1

Connect GitHub

Sign in with your GitHub account and authorize Jekyll Forge to access your repositories.

2

Select Your Blog

Choose your Jekyll repository from the list. We'll auto-detect your blog structure and settings.

3

Start Writing

Create, edit, and publish posts visually. Changes sync directly to GitHub—no command line needed.
