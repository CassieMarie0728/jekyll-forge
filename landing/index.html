Three Modes. One Mission: Publish Without Losing Your Mind.

Write visually, edit raw Markdown, or split the difference with live preview. Jekyll Forge adapts to the way your brain works that day, whether you are feeling organized or one browser tab away from combustion.

  • Visual Mode: Format, draft, and shape posts without memorizing every Markdown incantation.
  • Markdown Mode: Drop straight into the raw text when you want control and zero babysitting.
  • Split Preview: Edit on one side, preview on the other, and catch the weirdness before it goes live.
