Everything Your Blog Needed Before GitHub Started Swinging Chairs

A cleaner way to manage Jekyll content without sacrificing the power, portability, and beautiful nerd nonsense that makes static sites worth it.

📝

Visual Editor

Draft posts in a clean editor, then drop into Markdown when you want full control. No mystery formatting. No editor tantrums.

🔗

GitHub Native

Connect your repo, make changes, and publish through GitHub. Your content stays yours, not locked in some shiny SaaS basement.

✨

AI Writing Assistant

Use AI to tighten drafts, clean up clunky paragraphs, generate ideas, and make the page sound less like it was assembled by a sleep-deprived toaster.

🖼️

Asset Manager

Upload, organize, and place images without turning your media folder into a haunted junk drawer of final-final-actually-final files.

⏰

Scheduled Publishing

Queue posts before your brain wanders into traffic. Plan ahead, schedule the drop, and let the machine do the boring part.

👁️

Real-Time Preview

See the post before it escapes into the wild. Preview Markdown, front matter, layouts, and changes before GitHub gets a vote.

Three Modes. One Mission: Publish Without Losing Your Mind.

Write visually, edit raw Markdown, or split the difference with live preview. Jekyll Forge adapts to the way your brain works that day, whether you are feeling organized or one browser tab away from combustion.

  • Visual Mode: Format, draft, and shape posts without memorizing every Markdown incantation.
  • Markdown Mode: Drop straight into the raw text when you want control and zero babysitting.
  • Split Preview: Edit on one side, preview on the other, and catch the weirdness before it goes live.

How It Works Without the Ritual Sacrifice

Three steps. No smoke machine. No corporate wizardry. Just connect, choose, write, and ship.

1

Connect GitHub

Authorize Jekyll Forge so it can work with your repositories without making you manually drag files through the mud.

2

Pick Your Blog

Select the Jekyll repo you want to manage. The app reads the structure and gets out of your way.

3

Write and Ship

Create posts, preview changes, manage assets, and publish back to GitHub with a reviewable Git history.
