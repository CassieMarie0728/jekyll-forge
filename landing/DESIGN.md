# Landing design specification

Two generated concept sections define the design: editorial typewriter hero with a two-column Markdown demonstration; open feature rows, three steps, native FAQs and a left-ruled final CTA. No invented statistics, testimonials, app screenshots or paid-only requirements.

Tokens: #0b0f0f canvas; #981518 accents/buttons; #4e4b51 rules; #f5f5f5 headings; #a8a9ad body. Special Elite / Courier New. 52px desktop gutters, 20px mobile gutters, 104px section spacing, 2px button radii. Header navigation and hero text follow the concept.

Intentional deviations: white headline plus red underline and white smaller headings maintain contrast; demonstration explicitly labeled as an example; extra FAQ answers explain actual storage, hosting and documentation limits. No fictional app screenshot is used. All visible interaction is code-native. The layout stacks at 1100px and 600px instead of compressing text.
